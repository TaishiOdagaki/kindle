# Amazon Ads Strategy for the 登録日本語教員試験 Workbook Series

This is a sibling skill to `kdp-ads-strategy` (same accumulate-real-knowledge-over-time approach, same discipline of dated log entries over generic advice) but for a genuinely different product: study workbooks for the Registered Japanese Language Teacher (登録日本語教員) national certification exam, not the manga/anime criticism books. Both product lines run through the same "Sponsored ads - Author" Amazon Ads account, so search-term reports and dashboard screenshots can get mixed up between them — always confirm which book/campaign a screenshot or CSV actually belongs to before analyzing it (this happened once already: a manga-book search-term review turned out to be this product's data instead).

**Current title:** 登録日本語教員試験 決定版問題集 (the "decisive/definitive edition" workbook). A prior edition, marketed under a "パーフェクト2026" campaign, has since been discontinued/delisted — its ad data is historical reference only, not something to keep optimizing directly, but the keyword-level lessons from it still apply to the current edition.

## What we've learned (dated log — add to this, don't just read it)

- **2026-09-21 — analyzed the discontinued "パーフェクト2026" edition's full campaign history.** 371 impressions, 18 clicks, ¥850 spent, 3 orders, ¥1,365 sales, ROAS ≈ 1.61 over the campaign's life (2026-09-07 to 2026-09-12, ~5 days active). This is a real, converted campaign, not just noise — clear evidence the product category itself sells. Keyword-level breakdown was the useful part:
  - **Branded/specific terms converted well**: "日本語教員試験 パーフェクト" (1 click, ¥31, 1 order — converted on the very first click), a direct ASIN hit "B0GQ2WMMJH" (1 click, ¥6, 1 order — extremely cheap conversion), and "日本語教員試験 問題集" (4 clicks, ¥238, 1 order, ROAS 1.91) all converted.
  - **Broad/generic terms did not convert**: the bare term "日本語教員試験" alone (4 clicks, ¥148, 0 orders — highest volume of any term, zero return) and especially "登録日本語教員" (3 clicks, ¥220, 0 orders — the single worst cost-per-click of any term in the report, ~¥73/click, no conversions) burned real budget with nothing to show for it.
  - **Takeaway carried forward to the current edition**: include the current book's own distinguishing name/edition marker ("決定版") as an explicit keyword, the same way "パーフェクト" worked for the prior edition — specific, branded search intent converts far better than generic category browsing for this product. Watch "登録日本語教員" (the bare term, no exam/edition qualifier) closely in the current campaign; historically it's been the worst-performing term and a negative-keyword candidate if the pattern repeats.
- **2026-09-21 — current edition ("決定版問題集") campaign reviewed, still early.** 776 impressions, 13 clicks, ¥346 spent, 0 orders, over the campaign's first ~5 days (started 2026-09-16). Overall CTR ≈1.68%, healthy by Sponsored Products benchmarks. The dominant term so far is the bare "日本語教員試験" (504 impressions, 5 clicks alone) — the same generic term that never converted in the prior edition's data. No conversions yet, but volume is still low (13 clicks) and the prior edition also took several days and ~18 clicks before its first sale, so this isn't concerning on its own yet. Recommended: add "決定版" as an explicit keyword if not already targeted, and watch whether "登録日本語教員" (bare) repeats its poor performance from the prior edition — if so, negative-match it.
- **2026-09-25 — campaign ended (2026-09-16 to 2026-09-23); pulled the full-life numbers plus two search-term reports (9/16–9/20 and 9/21–9/25) to see what actually happened. Major reversal of the 09-21 working theory.** Final totals: 38 clicks, ¥1,279 total cost, 1 order, ¥682 sales, overall ROAS ≈ 0.53 (net loss campaign-wide, ACOS 187%). But the keyword-level breakdown tells a different story than the aggregate suggests:
  - **The bare term "日本語教員試験" — the one flagged twice before as a likely negative-match candidate — is what actually converted.** In the 9/21–9/25 report: 1,200 impressions, 13 clicks, ¥450.80 spent, **1 order, ¥682 sales, ROAS 1.51** on that keyword alone. It just needed real volume (13 clicks) to produce its first sale, exactly the "small sample, don't overreact" pattern this skill and `kdp-ads-strategy` both already warn about — except this time it was the *generic* term proving itself late, not a specific one. **The earlier negative-match recommendation for this term is retracted.** Two prior zero-conversion windows (4 clicks in the パーフェクト2026 data, 5 clicks in this edition's first 5 days) simply hadn't given it enough volume yet.
  - **What actually caused the campaign's net loss was auto-targeting's product-page-referral matching, not any keyword's own performance.** Several "search terms" in the reports were bare ASINs (4757444737, 4801110541, 4801110215, 4798076295, B0GYF6RS7Q, B0HJNJCQDS, etc.) — Amazon's auto-targeting showing the ad on similar/related product detail pages rather than against real typed searches. Combined, these burned roughly ¥320 across the two reports with zero conversions. This is a structural weakness of auto-targeting specifically (see the exact same failure mode already documented for the manga books' original auto-targeting campaigns) — moving to manual keyword targeting removes this category of waste entirely, it doesn't require picking better keywords.
  - **Generalizable lesson**: don't let one or two zero-conversion clicks on a bare/generic keyword trigger a negative-match decision — check the actual click count against a real threshold (the pattern seen twice now in this account is roughly "10+ clicks before a real generic-term conversion shows up," not the 3-5 clicks this skill originally judged on). A keyword with real impression volume (hundreds+) that simply hasn't converted yet in a small click sample is a fundamentally different situation from a keyword that never gets impressions in the first place — the latter (see `kdp-ads-strategy`'s 09-25 entry for the manga books' "companion book"-phrased keywords) really is dead; the former just needs patience.
- **2026-09-25 — launched a new campaign for 決定版問題集, manual targeting only, informed by the reversal above.** Auto-targeting dropped entirely (it was the actual source of the prior campaign's loss). Keyword list, deliberately narrowed rather than broad, each bid set from the actual observed CPC in the 9/21–9/25 report where available:
  - `日本語教員試験` — **完全一致 (exact match)**, ¥45 (vs. the ¥34.68 average CPC actually paid for this term in the prior campaign — bid set above the observed rate to keep winning comparable or better placement now that it has a proven conversion behind it)
  - `登録日本語教員試験` — **完全一致 (exact match)**, ¥35 (no direct CPC data exists for this exact phrase yet — the full official exam name, distinct from the bare "登録日本語教員" that performed worst historically — started conservative, watch and adjust)
  - `日本語教員試験 問題集` — **フレーズ一致 (phrase match)**, ¥40 (splits the difference between two inconsistent historical CPCs for this phrase: ¥59.50/click when it converted at ROAS 1.91 in the パーフェクト2026 data, vs. ¥33/click with no conversion in this edition's first 5 days)
  - Deliberately excluded: bare `登録日本語教員` (no "試験" suffix) — still the worst historical performer (~¥73/click, 0 orders, worst CPC of any term seen in this account) and, unlike the bare "日本語教員試験" reversal above, has no positive data point anywhere in this account's history to reconsider it on.
  - Daily budget raised to **¥600** (from ¥300) — reasoning: the bare exact-match term alone drove 1,200 impressions in under 5 days on the old campaign; a ¥300/day cap likely throttled delivery on the very term now being prioritized. Outcome of this campaign: not yet known.
- **2026-09-26 — early check-in on the new campaign: only "日本語教員試験" (bare, exact match) has any impressions so far, 362 of them; the other two keywords ("登録日本語教員試験" exact, "日本語教員試験 問題集" phrase) show none yet.** User applied Amazon's suggested bid, raising this keyword from ¥45 to **¥61** (~+36%), leaving the other two keywords untouched. Consistent with this account's now-repeated pattern: the bare, most generic term is the one that actually pulls real search volume, while the more specific/branded phrases (which converted better historically, per the 09-21 パーフェクト2026 breakdown) haven't shown any impressions yet in this campaign's first day or so — too early to read anything into that gap on its own. Worth watching at the next check-in: whether the ¥61 bid meaningfully changes this keyword's impression share/CTR versus its behavior at ¥45, and whether "登録日本語教員試験" and "日本語教員試験 問題集" start getting impressions once the campaign has had a few more days to run (both are exact/phrase match, which take longer to accumulate volume than a bare exact-match term already proven to have demand).
- **2026-09-27 — a morning sale (user-reported) initially looked missing from a pulled report; turned out to be a report-construction gap, not a real problem, and the corrected report shows healthy performance.** First pull (dimensions: only 広告主アカウント, no キャンペーン; metrics: impressions/clicks/CTR/CPC only, no orders/sales/ROAS) showed 723 impressions, 7 clicks, CTR 0.9682%, CPC ¥30.32 for 2026/09/25-09/26 — two problems at once: (1) no conversion metrics were included at all, so a real sale couldn't have shown up regardless of timing; (2) the only non-time dimension was account-level, not scoped to this campaign, risking the same cross-product blending this account has hit before (the manga companion books share this same "Sponsored ads - Author" account). **Corrected re-pull, this time with キャンペーン as a dimension and ROAS added**: same window, same campaign ("登録日本語教員試験　決定版問題集２") — 7 clicks, CTR 0.9722%, CPC ¥30.31571, **ROAS 3.21380**. The click count matching almost exactly between both pulls suggests this campaign's traffic wasn't meaningfully diluted by the other products in the first (uncorrected) pull, but the fix was still necessary and correct on principle — an account-wide, no-conversion-columns report is structurally unable to show whether a specific sale happened, independent of whether the number happens to come out the same. Back-of-envelope check: ¥30.31571 × 7 ≈ ¥212 cost; ¥212 × 3.2138 ≈ ¥682 sales — matches the known single-unit price point already seen in this account's other order data, consistent with one real conversion in this window (the user's reported morning sale). **ROAS 3.21 is comfortably healthy** on any reasonable reading, well above the ~1.5 breakeven line already established for the manga books (this product's own exact breakeven is still unconfirmed — see the open question below — but 3.21 has real margin even if this product's true breakeven runs somewhat higher). **No action taken or needed** — 7 clicks is still a small sample by this account's own "10+ clicks" pattern-confirmation habit, but there's no negative signal here to react to; recommended simply continuing to observe rather than touching bids/budget on a campaign that's currently working.

- **2026-09-28 — follow-up pull, one day after the last check-in: still healthy, sample size growing past the "10+ clicks" line.** "日本語教員試験" (bare, exact match, ¥61 bid) over 2026/09/25-09/27: 12 clicks, CTR 0.9836%, CPC ¥35.43083, **ROAS 3.20813** — up from 7 clicks/ROAS 3.21380 the day before, same window extended by one day. CPC rose from ¥30.32 to ¥35.43 as the ¥61 bid (raised 2026-09-26, from ¥45) worked its way into the blended average, but ROAS held essentially flat — a real, positive signal that the bid raise didn't erode margin, and this keyword has now crossed this account's own "10+ clicks before trusting a generic term" line while still reading healthy. "登録日本語教員試験" (the full-name exact-match keyword, ¥35 bid) still shows 0 clicks as of 09/26-09/27 — consistent with the 09-26 note that exact/phrase-match keywords take longer to accumulate volume than the bare term; not a concern yet on its own. **No action taken or needed** — this campaign is the clean opposite case to the three anime campaigns paused the same day (see `kdp-ads-strategy`'s 2026-09-28 entries): real clicks, real conversions, stable ROAS, nothing here points at a listing/reviews problem the way it does for those three.

- **2026-09-29 — CSV pull, one more day added: ROAS dipped from 3.21 to 2.04, but this is lumpy-sales noise, not a reversal.** Campaign-level totals for 2026/09/25-09/28: 19 clicks, CTR 1.0857%, CPC ¥35.15263, **ROAS 2.04222**. Back-of-envelope: cost ≈ 19 × ¥35.15263 ≈ ¥668; sales ≈ ¥668 × 2.04222 ≈ ¥1,364 — identical to the prior day's computed sales figure (12 clicks, ¥1,364 sales, 2 orders). **The 7 new clicks (12→19) added cost with zero new conversions; no new order landed in this window.** ROAS falling from a small-sample 3.2x toward a still-healthy 2.0x as click count grows is exactly the expected shape when sales arrive in discrete ¥682 lumps while cost accrues continuously per click — not a sign the campaign turned unprofitable. - **2026-10-01 — CSV pull (per-keyword dimension, not campaign-level): "日本語教員試験" (bare) has now gone 19 straight clicks with zero new conversions, and cumulative ROAS has dropped below the borrowed 1.5 breakeven line for the first time.** Two rows this pull: "登録日本語教員試験" (the full-name exact-match term) got its **first-ever click** (1 click, 2026/09/26-09/29, CTR 6.6667%, ROAS 0.00000) — too early to read (n=1), but a real milestone after sitting at 0 clicks since launch, consistent with exact/phrase-match terms taking longer to accumulate volume than the bare term. "日本語教員試験" (bare, exact match) over 2026/09/25-09/30: 31 clicks, CTR 1.0255%, CPC ¥36.04484, **ROAS 1.22070**. Back-of-envelope: cost ≈ 31 × ¥36.04484 ≈ ¥1,117; sales ≈ ¥1,117 × 1.2207 ≈ ¥1,364 — **the identical ¥1,364/2-order figure seen at both the 12-click and 19-click checkpoints.** This means the 12 additional clicks since the last pull (19→31) produced zero new conversions, extending the dry streak to 19 consecutive clicks with no sale (12→31) — longer than the ~10-13 click gap this account has previously treated as normal pre-first-conversion lag, and now a real dip below the reference breakeven line rather than just a falling-but-still-healthy number. **Two reasons not to cut the bid yet, given to the user**: (1) this product's own actual breakeven ROAS is still unconfirmed — 1.5 is borrowed from the $2.99/70%-royalty manga books, so "below 1.5" may not mean "below this product's real breakeven"; (2) sales have arrived in discrete lumps throughout this campaign's history, so a 19-click gap isn't yet statistically extreme enough to rule out normal variance. **Recommended next step: re-check after roughly 10-15 more clicks (not immediately) — if the drought continues that much further, reconsider the ¥61 bid on this keyword; if a new sale lands, this was just lumpiness again.** No action taken this session.

- **2026-10-02 — the dry streak hit the threshold flagged above and crossed a harder line: ROAS fell below 1.0, not just below the borrowed 1.5 breakeven.** "日本語教員試験" over 2026/09/25-10/01: 40 clicks, CTR 1.0638%, cost ¥1,562.50, 2 orders, sales ¥1,364.00, **ROAS 0.87296**. Reasoning: the 09-26 bid raise was justified by a suggested-bid figure backed by demand at the time, but the sustained post-raise performance didn't follow through, and the pre-exam demand increase (11-08 exam, still ~5 weeks out) isn't expected until late October, so there's no reason to hold a premium bid through a confirmed dry stretch waiting for seasonality that hasn't arrived yet.
