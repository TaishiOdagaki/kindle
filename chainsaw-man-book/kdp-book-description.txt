<h4>A Companion Built for the Moment After the Ending</h4>
<p><i>Chainsaw Man</i> is finished: twenty-four volumes, two parts, one deliberately uncomfortable ending. Which means it can finally be read whole, the way it was maybe always meant to be. This isn't a recap wiki walking you through what happens next. It's an argument for why it happens the way it does, chapter by chapter, written by someone who followed this story chapter by chapter too — honest about what worked and what didn't, not just hype.</p>

<h4>Designed, Not Just Written</h4>
<p>This isn't a wall of text. Every chapter opens with its own full-page image, built from the book's own cover art. Seven original data charts and diagrams — a spoiler-safe "what's inside" roadmap up front, a fear taxonomy mapping the story's own named monsters, a visual breakdown of Makima's control over Denji, a real publication timeline, more — sit right inside the arguments they illustrate, not tacked on as filler. You can see exactly how far you can read before it gets spoiler-heavy, before you've spent a dollar.</p>
